Social Media Design Campaign

Scroll-stopping promotional carousel layouts, display graphics, and branded engagement templates engineered to maximize audience retention across Instagram and LinkedIn.

01 • Overview

Project Overview & Background

In an ultra-competitive social media landscape where viewers make swipe decisions in less than 1.5 seconds, organic visual distinction is essential. This campaign framework was developed to deliver cohesive, high-impact social media creatives designed specifically for mobile feeds, story formats, and multi-slide carousel storytelling.

01

Feed Clutter

Generic stock templates blend into feeds, failing to capture user thumb-stops and resulting in poor audience engagement metrics.

02

Dynamic Hook Design

Crafting high-contrast headline blocks, 3D floating visual props, and seamless carousel slide crossovers that compel users to swipe.

03

Multi-Platform Asset Pack

Square 1:1 post templates (1080x1080), vertical 9:16 story/reel covers (1080x1920), and landscape LinkedIn feed banners (1200x628).

02 • Goals

Strategic Design Objectives

The visual strategy was structured around four core conversion and branding benchmarks:

1. Instant Visual Thumb-Stop

Utilizing neon accents against dark backgrounds and dynamic 3D depth to immediately arrest the viewer's scrolling momentum within the first second.

2. Frictionless Information Architecture

Structuring clear headline hierarchies so that key value propositions and promotional discounts are absorbed instantly even when skimming on small smartphone screens.

3. Continuous Carousel Pacing

Engineering connecting visual elements and bleed graphics across slide borders that naturally guide the viewer to swipe through all slides to the final CTA.

4. Brand Recall Consistency

Maintaining uniform logo placement, color proportions, and typography treatments across organic and paid ads to reinforce long-term brand equity.

03 • Creative Direction

Design Concept & Storytelling Mechanics

The design direction relies on dark-mode contrast, energetic 3D badges, and rhythmic visual pacing:

A

The Hero Hook Slide

Large bold typographic questions paired with high-energy 3D floating icons, sparking immediate curiosity without overcrowding the canvas.

B

Bite-Sized Value Delivery

Supporting slides break complex topics into numbered cards, high-contrast bullet points, and minimalist diagrams for effortless comprehension.

C

The Conversion Climax (CTA)

Final slide features high-visibility call-to-action buttons ("Save & Share", "Link in Bio", "DM for Inquiries") driving measurable audience action.

05 • Campaign Pipeline

Design Process & Workflow

A structured 4-phase creative workflow optimized for fast digital turnaround:

Phase 01

Concept & Content Mapping

Analyzing marketing goals, breaking down key messages into 5-to-7 slide sequences, and drafting hook headlines that target viewer pain points.

Phase 02

Wireframing & Slide Pacing

Laying out multi-artboard canvases in Adobe Photoshop to verify seamless graphic transitions and visual flow between adjacent slides.

Phase 03

Lighting, Textures & Effects

Applying custom glassmorphism panels, neon glows, drop shadows, and visual asset compositing to create rich, multi-layered depth.

Phase 04

Multi-Format Batch Export

Exporting crisp PNG-24 assets calibrated for Instagram compression algorithms, plus editable PSD master files for client reuse.
